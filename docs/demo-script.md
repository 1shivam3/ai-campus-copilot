# CoursePilot Demonstration Scripts

This guide provides structured demonstration flows for presenting CoursePilot to evaluators, technical interviewers, and prospective users.

---

## 1. The 30-Second Pitch (Elevator Flow)

> *"Most students use static to-do lists that treat a minor homework sheet due in two weeks the same as an urgent midterm exam scheduled in three days. CoursePilot is an AI-powered academic co-pilot that replaces decision fatigue with a deterministic Next Best Action engine. It continuously evaluates your university timetable, active assignment deadlines, exam proximity, and topic mastery curves to tell you exactly what deserves your attention right now—and why."*

## 2. The 2-Minute Standard Walkthrough

### Step 1: Landing Page & Problem Context (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Action**: Open [https://ai-campus-copilot-one.vercel.app/](https://ai-campus-copilot-one.vercel.app/).
- **Narration**: *"Here is CoursePilot. Instead of giving students a generic list of checkboxes, we provide a unified decision system."*
- **Key Visual**: Highlight the Comparison Card contrasting a traditional to-do list with CoursePilot's synthesized academic reasoning.

### Step 2: Dashboard & Next Best Action (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Action**: Log in to access the Dashboard.
- **Narration**: *"When a student logs in, the dominant element is their Next Best Action. Notice the 'Why this now?' explainability breakdown. CoursePilot tells the student: 'Your Discrete Mathematics exam is in 3 days, Relations is currently your weakest syllabus topic at 35% mastery, and this 45-minute task fits perfectly into your free study window before your next lecture at 2:00 PM.' No guessing required."*

### Step 3: Deep Work Focus Session & Feedback Loop (1:00 - 1:30)
- **Action**: Click **Start Now** on the Next Best Action card $\to$ Launch Focus Session $\to$ Click **✓ Mark Task Done & Return to Dashboard**.
- **Narration**: *"The student clicks 'Start Now' and enters a distraction-free Pomodoro session with context already selected. When they finish and mark it complete, two things happen immediately: their topic mastery score increments in the database, and the engine recalculates priorities in real-time, removing the completed task and surfacing the next critical priority without a page reload."*

### Step 4: AI Study Pack & Exam Mode Strategy (1:30 - 2:00)
- **Action**: Navigate to **Study Material** or **Exam Mode** $\to$ Show on-demand Gemini AI Study Pack and Exam Practice Quiz.
- **Narration**: *"Whenever the student needs guidance, our backend AI proxy synthesizes their lecture notes via RAG to formulate high-yield study packs, SM-2 flashcards, and adaptive exam practice MCQs."*

## 4. Key Technical Talking Points Cheat-Sheet

1. **Why deterministic scoring instead of raw LLM prioritization?**
   - LLMs can hallucinate dates, give inconsistent priority rankings between sessions, and introduce unnecessary API latency and costs. CoursePilot calculates priorities deterministically via mathematically bounded algorithms, using Gemini strictly for content synthesis, revision advice, and quiz generation.

2. **How does CoursePilot protect user data?**
   - PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) checks `auth.uid() = user_id` on every query. Direct database manipulation across accounts is rejected at the database engine level.

3. **How does the system handle AI reliability and rate limits?**
   - The FastAPI backend implements a cascading multi-model fallback across 5 Gemini Flash model tiers (`gemini-flash-latest`, `gemini-3.5-flash`, `gemini-flash-lite-latest`, etc.) with input sanitization and Pydantic length bounds.
